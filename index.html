What's the difference between a prompt and an AI skill?

A prompt is one message. A skill is a saved, tested setup inside your AI tool, with instructions, your examples, and your brand voice built in. You run it the same way every time and get consistent, client-ready output instead of a new surprise every day.

Which AI tools do you work with?

Claude (Projects and Skills), ChatGPT (Custom GPTs), and Google Gemini (Gems). I build inside the tool you already pay for, so there's no new software to buy.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. I map your process, build the skill, and test it with your real work. You get a plain-language guide, two recorded training sessions, and 30 days of async support. If you can copy and paste, you can run your skills.

Who owns the skills when we're done?

You do. Every skill, prompt, and page of documentation is yours. The goal is a business you can run without me, not a business stuck waiting on me.
